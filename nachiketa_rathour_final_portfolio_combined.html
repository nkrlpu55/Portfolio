01 / Flagship Case Study

Selected Work

A content-operations platform built around assignment flow, performance logic, role-aware access and operational continuity.

Content Operations Platform

QUACKER

Content Operations & Performance Management Platform

QUACKER grew from the practical demands of high-volume editorial work. The system structures task assignment, submission tracking, turnaround scoring, role-based views and a lightweight operational fallback.

ReactNext.jsFirebaseGoogle Apps ScriptGoogle Sheets
Chapter 01The problem

High-volume editorial work needed a clearer operating layer.

Manual spreadsheet workflows made assignment status, turnaround and performance harder to track consistently. QUACKER was conceived as a central operating layer for the workflow rather than another document to maintain.

Before

Fragmented operational tracking

Assignments, submissions, status changes and performance information were difficult to see as one connected workflow.

After

One structured workflow

Task assignment, submission state, scoring and role-aware views are brought together in one operational interface.

Chapter 05Access model

Different people see different parts of the operation.

The screenshots show separate administrator and contributor experiences. The system concept extends this into role-aware access for administrators, managers and team members.

ADMIN

Task assignment, operational data and team-level oversight.

MANAGER

03 / Automation & Logic

Digital Systems

Practical workflow automation using Google Sheets and Apps Script, alongside organic distribution systems.

Before automation

Manual operating state

  • Status updatesTurnaround states required repeated manual maintenance.
  • CountingContent volume and operational totals required manual tallying.
  • ReportingRecurring management views involved repetitive consolidation.
After automation

Structured operating state

  • Automated transitionsApps Script can respond to defined sheet events and workflow conditions.
  • Automated countersFormula-based and scripted tracking reduces repetitive counting.
  • Structured reportingInformation can be organised into repeatable operational views.
Supporting practice

Reddit organic strategy

Community research, discussion framing and contextual educational resource sharing without intrusive promotional messaging.

The broader principle

Structure before scale.

The common thread across the work is simple: understand the information first, then design the operating structure around it.
